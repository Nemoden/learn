---
description: Queue a topic to learn later in to-learn.md
---

Append the topic to `to-learn.md` with today's date in the format:

```
- [YYYY-MM-DD] <topic to explore>
```

These are topics encountered during learning but deferred for later (could be advanced topics, alternative approaches, or interesting tangents).

Examples:
- `- [2025-12-01] OAuth Device Authorization Grant (for CLI tools and IoT devices)`
- `- [2025-12-01] Macaroons (bearer tokens with embedded caveats)`
- `- [2025-12-01] SPIFFE/SPIRE for workload identity`
